# Incident brief format

Use this structure for the brief assembled in step 3, and read it only when building or reviewing the brief.

## Sections

1. Header: the location, the reported time window, and when the brief was produced.
2. Event list: each event on its own line, in time order, with its time, its camera, its event kind, and its familiar-or-unknown label.
3. Evidence: for each event, the one snapshot or clip that the monitoring step returned.
4. Narrative summary: two to four plain sentences that describe the sequence in order, using only the events that were actually returned.

## Rules

- Keep every event tied to a real returned record; never add, merge, or reorder beyond time order.
- Label a person or vehicle as unknown whenever the NAS could not decide; never guess a name.
- Mark the brief as partial when an earlier step failed or was cancelled.
- Keep the whole brief short enough to read in under a minute.

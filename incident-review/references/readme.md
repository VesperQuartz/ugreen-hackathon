# Incident Review — Overview

## Who it is for

On-site security staff, facility managers, and small-business owners who already
run cameras on a UGREEN AI NAS and need to understand a reported incident quickly.

## What it does

You describe a location and a rough time window. The Skill reviews the NAS's own
recorded events for that window, orders them, labels each person or vehicle as
familiar or unknown, attaches one snapshot or clip per event, and writes a short
brief. With your confirmation, the finished brief is saved as a note.

## How to use it

Trigger it in plain language. For example: something was moved in the storeroom
this afternoon, what happened. The Skill asks for the location and the time window
if they are missing, then runs the review.

## What it does not do

It does not name a person the NAS has not already learned, it does not search
footage the NAS did not record, and it never invents events. If it finds nothing,
it says so and stops.

## Requirements

A UGREEN AI NAS with Surveillance Center, at least one managed camera, and local
AI detection turned on. The AI event index covers a limited recent window, so it
is best to investigate soon after an incident.

## Removal

Delete the Skill folder to remove it. It keeps nothing of its own beyond the notes
you choose to save.
